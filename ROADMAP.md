# Comparative SAR Image Reconstruction under Noise, Limited Aperture, and Platform Motion Errors

## Project Roadmap

### Project Objective

Develop a MATLAB-based simulation framework to compare Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) image reconstruction algorithms under realistic operating conditions including additive noise, limited aperture, and platform motion errors.
